  * the event pump invokes `python3 -m egg_agent` (NOT the raw CLI
    print-mode entrypoint) per the EGG100 ban codified at
    docs/guides/agent-mode-design.md:90-104;
  * the pump processes PROPOSE, ACK and CONFIRMED events in order,
    exits cleanly after each event (no STAY-ALIVE), and terminates 0
    on CONSENSUS_CONFIRMED without restarting the agent.

and the wall-clock / cache-cost numbers captured by
